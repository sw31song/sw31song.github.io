## About Me

I am an environmental and occupational health researcher with an MPH in Environmental and Occupational Health Sciences. My research focuses on exposure assessment, airborne pollutants, and the health effects of environmental and occupational exposures.

My research experience includes aerosol characterization, workplace exposure assessment, and household air pollution monitoring. I am particularly interested in understanding how personal exposures vary over time and developing methods to better estimate exposure and evaluate its health effects.

## Research Interests

- Occupational and environmental exposure assessment
- Air pollution and ultrafine particles
- Exposure measurement and modeling
- Short-term health effects of airborne pollutants
- Exposure reduction and prevention strategies
